# Changelog

## 0.1.9 — unreleased

- Vehicle gunners shoot on the move, and their crew calls targets out. An armoured vehicle sent on a hurried move (AC: Actual's fast moves, or any order raised to the level of a player's) drove past enemies its gunner could see without firing a shot: the game ranked the move above everything the gunner did, so he only opened up once the vehicle stopped. Now the vehicle still goes where it was sent, and the gunner fires on the way. And a gunner who has spotted nothing himself now takes his crew's word for it: when the driver or commander sees enemies, the gunner swings onto the biggest group of them (ten men at 300 m before one man at 80 m, men shooting at the vehicle first) and puts a burst of fire on them until he spots them himself. In a test of a LAV-25 driving past an enemy fire team, the gunner fired 72 rounds on the way instead of none; with the gunner unable to see, his crew's call-out had him firing 16 rounds on the way instead of none. Hold fire still holds him, and a Game Master's or a player's own squad orders are left alone. Options: `-bbbGunnerOrders 0` and `-bbbCrewCallout 0` (or the Battle Buddies settings tab, Vehicles and crews).
- Tank commanders and loaders sitting inside a mod tank's turret are crew, not passengers. Some mod vehicles (the Chieftain, Leopard 1, T-55 and FV510 Warrior from the Zagoria packs) seat them in what the game counts as a passenger seat, so when a crew got its passengers out under fire, the commander and loader climbed out of their tank too. Seats like the LAV-25 commander's, wired to a turret, already counted as crew.
- Fire discipline: soldiers stop emptying their magazines at range and get into cover again. Two squads that saw each other at 200 m used to spray most of their ammo and hit almost nothing, and men under fire from an enemy they couldn't see fired back from where they stood instead of taking cover: Better Battle Buddies' answer to an unseen shooter ranked above the game's own reaction, which is the one that runs a soldier into cover. Now a soldier shot at by someone he can't see gets into cover first, then fires a short burst from there; only two or three men of a squad fire back like that at once, riflemen only within 250 m and machine gunners within 500 m, and nobody uses his last spare magazines on it. Past 100 m everyone fires shorter bursts and aims longer, down to single aimed shots from rifles at 300 m, and suppressing a group they have spotted stops past 350 m (600 m for machine guns). In a test of two rifle squads holding at 200 m, they fired 983 rounds in two and a half minutes instead of 1,696 and spent seven times as long in cover; at 400 m, 146 rounds instead of 843. Option: `-bbbFireDiscipline 0` brings back the old rules (or the Battle Buddies settings tab, Awareness and aim).

- Medics no longer empty the squad's smoke grenades over a man snagged on barbed wire. Medic smoke went up for any casualty waiting for a heal while the squad was under threat, whatever had hurt him, so one soldier caught in the wire in front of a fortification drew every smoke grenade the squad carried. Medic smoke now needs a wound from the enemy (a bullet, blast, fragment, fire or a blow from another man) in the last ten minutes, and a squad only fires back at where a hit came from when that hit came from the enemy in the last ten seconds. Wire, falls, crashes and bleeding never count.

## 0.1.8 — released

- Works with Arma Reforger 1.9. In 1.9 the game's automatic gearbox takes the gear and throttle from the driver's own controls every moment, so whenever Better Battle Buddies drove a vehicle for its AI driver (backing a truck away from an incoming rocket or an armed vehicle, backing a stuck vehicle out, pulling over onto the verge) the game put it straight back in neutral: the truck sat still or crept forward. Better Battle Buddies now takes the driver's controls for the few seconds it drives and hands them back as soon as it's done.
- Loads with long mod lists. Every loaded mod shares one engine limit on the script set-up that runs as the game loads, and with a big stack (ACE, a content pack, AiO and more) it ran over and the game refused to load its scripts. Better Battle Buddies now does that set-up once the game has started instead, so it no longer counts against the limit.
- A frag that is still waiting to be thrown is called off if a friendly soldier or player walks into where it would land, or into its path. The blast and path were checked when the throw was decided, but a throw can wait a few seconds behind what the soldier is already doing, and nobody looked again before he let go. He still never drops a throw that is already under way.
- A soldier picked to take over an empty turret is left alone if things change before he gets there: a player takes him over or takes a seat in that vehicle, he gets a player's or Game Master's order, he changes squad, or the host mod takes the vehicle back. Before, the seating went ahead on the earlier decision; it also switched on the AI of a soldier that something else had deliberately parked.
- Fixed a rare script error when a soldier's squad was still being set up (seen once on a hosted game with AiO Command Menu loaded): the check for whether his squad holds fire read a part of the squad that wasn't there yet.
- Squads pulled back under fire fall back properly instead of going to ground. A host mod (AC: Actual's commander) that orders a squad back while it is being shot at used to leave it fighting where it stood: in a test, a team given a plain order to pull back 130 m got 5 m in a minute and spent half of it lying in the open. Now, while the enemy is in sight, the squad bounds back in two halves: one runs toward the spot it was sent to while the other covers it, then they swap; with nobody in sight both run. In the same test the team got 57-70 m back in a minute and nobody lay down who should have been running. Used by AC: Actual's covered fall-back; does nothing on its own. For mod makers: a host asks for this with the new `BBB_EIntent.WITHDRAW` (where to, and how close counts); `BBB_Morale.OrderedOn()` says whether it is switched on. Option: `-bbbWithdrawOrder 0` turns it off (or the Battle Buddies settings tab, Morale).
- For mod makers: `BBB_Terrain` knows rivers and ponds. `WaterDepthAt`, `IsDeepWaterAt` and `DeepWaterAlong` read the game's water surfaces (sea, river, pond); the older `IsWater` only knew ground below sea level, so inland rivers read as dry land. `BridgeNear` finds the road bridge over a stretch of deep water: where its road is back on dry ground at each end (a viaduct runs on far past the water) and the way onto it along its road.
- Fixes for the vehicle options (off by default):
  - A vehicle pulling over (giving way, or a host's get-out) now reaches the side of the road: the spot is one it can drive to (the verge, else the shoulder, else the road's own edge, never a ditch), and the wheel stays short of full lock, where a heavy 4x4 crawled at walking pace.
  - An unarmed vehicle backing away from an armed enemy ahead brakes to a stop first. It used to speed up instead: the gearbox refuses reverse at speed.
- Hot AI, two new options, both off by default. **Spotting and fire timing** (`-bbbHotAI`): AI soldiers spot faster and keep their eyes when shot at (the game drops a soldier's spotting to 0.4 of normal under fire, so men behind sandbags went blind exactly when they were attacked), shoot sooner at a new target, and fire longer bursts with shorter aiming pauses. **Gunners and vehicles** (`-bbbHotVehicles`): turret gunners get time to bring the gun onto a target, hold a target they can see instead of switching every half second, feel hits on their vehicle, put fire on the target they lost sight of, on an enemy who shot at them or on what their crewmates see, use the main gun against infantry, re-attack a vehicle they only stared at, follow a moving vehicle by its engine noise and reload an empty turret gun from the vehicle's stores; an AI-driven armed vehicle that is hit slows down. Each is in the Settings tab (Awareness and aim), in presets, a launch parameter, and a checkbox in a Game Master's game settings for the rest of the mission. The tuning values stay as they are and only act while their option is on.
- For mod makers: `BBB_SpawnValues.Skill` and `BBB_SpawnValues.Perception` say what the Settings tab sets for every AI soldier, so a host mod that sets skill and spotting itself can let them through.
- New option, off by default: **Return fire at unseen shooters** (`-bbbReturnFire 1`, the Battle Buddies settings tab, Awareness and aim). A soldier shot at from behind a bush or out of the dark never sees his shooter, so the game only had him look and get away from the fire: its own suppressive fire on unseen shooters ranks below both, and starts only past 30 m. Now a soldier on foot who has a round pass close or is hit, by an enemy he can't see, fires a short burst back toward where the shots came from (as well as he can place them by ear), then takes cover as usual. Not under hold fire, a player's or Game Master's order, in a vehicle or at shots from the sky.
- New vehicle options (the Battle Buddies settings tab, Vehicles and crews); giving way and getting away are off by default, the handbrake is on:
  - **Vehicles give way** (`-bbbGiveWay 1`). Two friendly AI vehicles on a collision course agree who gives way: a player's vehicle, then an armed one, then the heavier, keep going. Head-on, the one giving way pulls onto its right verge and waits while the other passes slowly; on any other course it brakes and waits. Never for an enemy. Before, both swerved or both stopped, and vehicles turning round at a base drove into each other.
  - **Handbrake when the driver leaves** (on; `-bbbHandbrake 0` turns it off). A vehicle its AI driver has left gets its handbrake on, so it doesn't roll away on a slope: from Arma Reforger 1.9 an empty vehicle drops into neutral, and one left on a hill rolls off on its own. The game sets it only when the whole crew gets out through the normal order. A driver who leaves under fire sometimes forgets.
  - **Unarmed vehicles get away from armed enemies** (`-bbbEvadeArmour 1`). An unarmed AI vehicle whose crew sees an armed enemy vehicle within 350 m asks the host mod to drive it away. Without a host mod that does, nothing changes.
  - For mod makers: `BBB_PullOver` drives a vehicle onto its verge (`MODE_PARK`) for a host that wants it off the road before a get-out, and the new `BBB_Hooks.Evade` is where a host takes an unarmed vehicle away.
- AI soldiers throw grenades and fire launchers at enemy players again. The check that keeps friendlies out of a blast counted every player as a friend before looking at his side, and the target's own spot is where the grenade lands, so a player was never grenaded or rocketed: not in a building, behind a wall or dug in. Players are now judged by their side like any soldier; a friendly player (or one whose side can't be read) is still kept clear.
- A gunner whose static gun is out of ammo gets off it and fights on foot with his rifle. The game never takes an AI gunner off an empty gun, so he sat on it doing nothing; held guns (a host mod's checkpoint or nest) were worse, since BBB kept their gunners on. Now a gunner on any static gun of an AI squad (a host mod's or a Game Master's) gets off once it has been dry for a few seconds, a dry held gun is no longer held or manned again, and it is manned again once it has ammo. Mortars are left alone (their shells are loaded by hand). Player and Game Master orders still win, and a group on a Game Master's get-in waypoint stays put. Option: `-bbbGunWinchester 0` (or the Battle Buddies settings tab, Static weapons).
- Smoke counts as cover only once it's really there. A squad or medic smoke throw counted as a screen from the moment it was ordered, so if the thrower was shot or knocked down before the grenade left his hand, medics still treated the planned spot as screened and walked casualties through open ground, and the squad waited out its smoke cooldown behind nothing. Now a throw counts only once a smoke grenade has actually left the thrower, and the screen is where that grenade lies, not where it was aimed. A throw that never happens counts for nothing.
- Soldiers on the wrong side of a sandbag wall no longer count as in cover. The game says a soldier is in cover once he reaches his cover spot, even when that spot is on the enemy's side of the wall, so men stood in front of the sandbags, hid there when pinned down, and never moved. Now cover only counts when it is between the soldier and the enemy he is fighting: one on the wrong side moves behind the wall (or to other cover nearby that faces the enemy), and if there is none he fights from where he is like any soldier in the open. Buildings are unchanged. Option: `-bbbTrueCover 0` (or the Battle Buddies settings tab, Movement).
- With ACE Carrying loaded, the player hosting a game (or playing singleplayer) could no longer walk forward or sideways while an AI soldier dragged a casualty to cover: W, A and D did nothing until the drag ended (S and looking around still worked). ACE limits a dragging soldier's walking by holding back the keyboard on the machine that runs him, and AI soldiers run on the host. BBB now switches that limit off for AI draggers; a player who drags someone still gets ACE's normal limit.
- Soldiers get off the road in a firefight. A soldier shot at while on a road found no cover on the open tarmac, so he lay down where he stood; two squads meeting on a road lay on the same strip and traded shots until one side was gone. Now a soldier on foot who stops on a road in a fight moves off it to the side he is nearer, into cover there if there is any, otherwise onto the verge. On a bridge or an overpass he stays (the verge there is a drop into a river or a ravine), and a verge in a river, pond or the sea is never picked. Player and Game Master orders still win. Option: `-bbbRoadExit 0` (or the Battle Buddies settings tab, Movement).
- Machine gunners and AT gunners now work with the man who carries their ammunition. The assistant keeps close to his gunner (one who drifts more than 20 m away walks back to him), walks over and hands ammunition across when the gunner reaches his last spare magazine or rocket instead of the gunner running dry or going to search bodies, and picks up the launcher if his gunner falls. Teams are recognised from what each soldier carries, so modded factions work too (-bbbSpecialistTeams 0 turns it off).
- AT gunners use their launchers on infantry. The game keeps every rocket for vehicles, so a gunner with an HE round never fired it at men. Now, while no enemy armour is known, he fires HE, dual-purpose or thermobaric rockets at a group or a man behind cover, and any rocket at a man in a building or dug in behind solid cover, 25 to 300 m away (200 with a disposable). Never at players, under hold fire, or with friendlies near where it lands. A gunner carrying both kinds swaps to the round that suits the target when it's safe, without dropping the one in the tube, and walks with the tube empty out of contact, then loads for the first target. Options: `-bbbLauncherInfantry 0`, `-bbbLauncherSwap 0`, `-bbbLauncherCombatLoad 0`, `-bbbLauncherDugIn 0` (or the Battle Buddies settings tab).
- For mod makers: a host mod now tells Better Battle Buddies one thing per squad, what it wants from it in a fight: push (it has ground to take), hold (a post), transit (riding, forming up, forced moves) or leave alone. Squad tactics and fire and manoeuvre both follow that answer, so a squad is never allowed to plan an attack but not to move. A pushing squad holds only when outnumbered or flanked, and for at most 90 seconds before its order carries it on; the host can read what each squad is doing, whether it is gaining ground and whether it needs support. Without a host mod nothing changes: the squad's waypoint decides as before (-bbbHostIntent 0 brings back the older separate rules).
- Squads attacking an objective keep the attack moving. The squad tactic took ordinary return fire for being pinned down: the game's own "threat" reading rises with every shot a soldier hears, his own squad's included, and as soon as he has someone to aim at, so a squad trading fire with the enemy read as pinned, stopped advancing and held for 45 seconds. Now only real incoming fire (rounds snapping past, blasts) counts as pinned. A squad with ground to take (a search and destroy, attack or move order) holds only when it is outnumbered or flanked; otherwise it keeps following its order and returns fire as it goes. Fire and manoeuvre also stopped treating men as too shaken to bound just because their own team was firing (-bbbTactic 0 turns squad tactics off).
- Better Battle Buddies now restarts cleanly for every mission. Without a host mod (Conflict, Game Master, scenario missions), a server that restarted or rotated its scenario, or the next mission in singleplayer, kept running BBB on the previous mission's state: illumination flares stayed off and horizon scanning skipped most groups for as long as the previous mission had lasted, and a new preset's vehicle and order settings weren't read. BBB now stops when a mission ends and starts fresh with the next one.
- Lighter on the server: gunner replacement checks whether a turret needs a gunner before anything else, and the door, weapon-lowering and head-scanning checks skip work for soldiers they can't affect. Checking whether a soldier is a player no longer walks the whole player list, which cost more the fuller the server.
- Fixed a script error when a soldier was removed while waiting for a window to fight from (seen in a long AC: Actual battle).
- Soldiers told to hold fire no longer throw hand grenades. Every other BBB fighting behaviour already respected hold fire, but the grenade throw didn't, so a squad waiting in ambush, or one a Game Master or player had set to hold fire, could give itself away with a frag.
- Squads now lose heart when they take losses. Morale was meant to count casualties and empty magazines, but the sums rounded to zero while anyone was still alive, so a squad down to two men of eight fought on as if fresh. Heavy losses now push a squad towards falling back or surrendering, as intended (-bbbMorale 0 turns morale off).
- Player and Game Master orders are respected in more places: a soldier under an order is no longer pulled into a vehicle's empty turret, and a Game Master's heal order is carried out instead of being turned into a drag to cover. A medic's covering smoke follows the same rules as squad smoke: never from a player's squad, and not when the host mod forbids smoke.
- A soldier who is dragging a casualty, running to treat one or busy with another job is no longer sent to man an empty held gun.
- A vehicle being backed out of a dead end when BBB stops (a host mod turning it off, a mission ending) gets its driver's AI back instead of sitting parked for good, and no longer switches on a driver that something else had parked.
- The thinking panel no longer says a squad dismounted under fire, or bailed out of a stopped vehicle, every second when nobody actually got out.
- The "Smoke on one spot cooldown" setting now works: soldiers wait that long before smoking the same place again (before, any spot could be smoked again as soon as the last cloud cleared).
- A gate that can't be opened from the seat is handled the game's way again (a crewman gets out and opens it), instead of the vehicle waiting at a shut gate.
- Soldiers who change squads (a Game Master regroup, a merge, a recruit) no longer carry the old squad's state with them: a man moved out of a holding squad advances again, a gunner leaves the old squad's static gun, and a squad that a player takes over during a fall-back stops falling back instead of being stuck half-way.
- Turret gunners under a player or Game Master order, in a player's squad, or in a vehicle the host mod leaves alone no longer fire bursts at a heard threat on their own.
- A soldier stuck beside a door that swinging doesn't free stops being retried for ever. After two rounds of swings he is set a few metres along his own path, further each time; after three of those he and the door are left alone for five minutes. Before, one door was swung 237 times in an hour for one man, who never got past it.
- Soldiers looking around when BBB stops (a host mod turning it off mid-mission) turn their heads back instead of staying in free-look.
- Morale now works on big missions. Only the first 64 AI squads in the world ever got a morale check, so on a busy Conflict or Game Master mission the squads actually fighting often never lost heart, fell back or surrendered while idle garrisons held every slot.
- A squad riding in a truck no longer tries to fall back while still seated, and a soldier who has surrendered is no longer ordered to run off with his squad. A soldier with a loaded rifle no longer counts as out of ammo just because his empty launcher is still in his hands.
- A player's heal command to his AI medic is carried out. Before, the medic stayed put while he was pinned down or could see an enemy, or turned the order into a drag to cover; only a Game Master's order was obeyed straight away. A player who plays a medic is also no longer picked over the AI for a heal his agent can't carry out.
- The medic's covering smoke is never thrown by a squadmate who is under a player's or Game Master's order, dragging a casualty or busy with another job.
- The "Smoke squad cooldown" setting works above 120 seconds (before, a squad could smoke again after 120 seconds whatever it was set to).
- Launch parameters are read the same way as the Settings tab: `-bbbSmoke true`, `-bbbGrenades on` or a parameter given without a value no longer switch a feature off, and numbers are kept inside their range.
- The "Gates and doors" and "Unstick vehicles" switches turn off everything they describe: AI drivers no longer open gates ahead, and soldiers on truck beds are no longer set down, when they are off (the built-in Vanilla preset included).
- A player driving a vehicle with AI riding in the back keeps his passengers until he lets them out: BBB no longer unloads them when he slows down under fire (they also wouldn't board his vehicle again), and no longer closes his doors.
- A held gun whose gunner is killed or knocked out is manned again. The body in the seat counted as a gunner, so the gun stayed silent for the rest of the fight.
- A vehicle being backed out of a dead end no longer stays steered by BBB once a player (or a Game Master possessing the driver) takes the wheel, and a manoeuvre dropped by a host mod gives the driver his AI back instead of leaving him frozen in the seat.
- A soldier knocked out on his way to a static gun no longer walks to it on his own once revived.
- Squads searching for a lost enemy check the spots nearest where he was last seen first, instead of jumping to the farthest one after the first.
- Player-led squads keep the game's own illumination flares at night; BBB had switched them off without firing its own.
- Without a host mod and with `-bbbVanilla 0`, medic and casualty-drag state is cleared between missions, so medics keep watching their casualties in the next mission.
- Vehicle gunners stop their bursts at a heard threat as soon as their squad is told to hold fire, and a frag that hasn't left the hand yet is put away on hold fire or a new order.
- Soldiers no longer throw a frag when a player or a soldier from another friendly squad stands between them and the target (before, only their own squad was checked).
- A launcher gunner who picked a rocket for an enemy soldier holds it if, while he reloads, the man gets too close or friendlies move in on him.
- AI in a player's squad are no longer thrown to the ground by BBB's explosion and armour reflexes; the player's squad is left to the player.
- A driver killed while BBB was lining his vehicle up for the gunner no longer has his AI switched back on.
- A gunner stays on his gun when the vehicle has no driver. Before, a gun truck or armoured car parked with only its gunner (a Game Master checkpoint, say), or one whose driver was killed, was abandoned by everyone aboard as soon as an enemy was known nearby, and the squad never used it again. Now only the riders get out to fight on foot, and a Game Master's order keeps everyone where he put them.
- Soldiers shoot at an armoured vehicle's crew when they stand in the hatches. Once riflemen had learned their rounds couldn't hurt an APC, they also held fire at its commander with his head out. Now that lesson covers the hull only, lasts five minutes before they try again, and is forgotten when the mission ends.
- Squads under fire from a vehicle or a static gun alone now know where the fire is coming from: they react, take cover behind smoke and fall back away from it. Before, BBB's picture of the fight only counted enemy soldiers, so a squad hit by a BTR or a technical behaved as if there were no enemy at all.

## 0.1.7 — released

- A **Battle Buddies** tab in the game's Settings menu, so you can set up how BBB's AI fights without touching launch parameters. Every option has a switch or slider with a short description, grouped by feature. Save setups as presets, then duplicate, rename, delete and share them: Export copies a preset to the clipboard and tells you where its file is. Two built-in presets to start from: BBB default (what the mod ships) and Vanilla (every BBB feature off). A preset applies when a mission starts, in singleplayer and in games you host; on someone else's server, that server's settings apply. Server hosts drop the file into the server's profile folder under BBB/Presets and start the server with -bbbPreset <name>. Launch parameters still win over a preset, so existing server setups keep working unchanged.
- New options for values that used to be fixed: how long squads assault, hold and reorganise; how far a squad without a radio can call out a contact, and how fresh a sighting must be to pass on; the grenade throwing lane; smoke and illumination cooldowns; the morale breaking point and how far a broken squad falls back. Also two of the game's own AI values, skill and spotting speed, set when a soldier spawns; anything a host mod, Game Master or mission sets later still wins. Every new option defaults to today's behaviour.
- Soldiers fighting from a building stay in it. Before, the game's combat movement sent a soldier who'd stood still for a few seconds to cover 10-30 m towards the enemy, and its reaction to being shot at sent him sprinting in a random direction: both meant out of the door into the open, so garrisons emptied within seconds of the first shot. Now a soldier inside keeps his window while he can see the enemy, moves to another window in the same building when he can't, ducks behind the walls when shot at instead of running out, and stands back up to see over the sill instead of lying blind on the floor. Defenders hold even when they lose sight of the enemy; others go after him as before if the building gives them no view. An enemy inside the same building is still fought room to room (-bbbIndoorHold 0).
- Soldiers no longer throw frag grenades from inside buildings or into a wall or overhang just in front of them. The old check only looked 3 m straight ahead, but a grenade is lobbed, so it could hit the ceiling or the wall above a window and land at the thrower's feet.
- Squads routed on foot round a bay or lake keep a little inland instead of hugging the waterline, and no leg of the route ends on a beach. Before, a leg could end right at the water's edge, where the game's pathfinding sometimes fails every move and the squad stood there for good (-bbbShoreRoutes 0).
- Armour learning judges each round fired, not each hit. Before, it could decide in seconds that a 25 mm cannon or an M72 couldn't hurt a BTR-70 they were wrecking, and gunners stopped firing at it: it judged every spall fragment as a hit of its own and only counted damage to the exact part struck, so wheels, engines and gearboxes destroyed behind the hull counted for nothing, and the 25 mm's spall was credited to other rounds. Now a round's spall, fragments and blast all count as that round, and it got through if the vehicle lost health anywhere or someone inside was hurt. Rifle rounds that do nothing to armour are still learned as useless (-bbbArmour 0).
- For mod makers: a host mod can give a squad its own leash, a circle it may manoeuvre in (an objective, a post). While it is set it replaces the order leash for every Better Battle Buddies behaviour, and fire and manoeuvre may then run under a Defend waypoint, inside that circle only. Nothing changes for squads without one, so Conflict and Game Master play as before.
- A vehicle crewman that Better Battle Buddies turned out of his hatch goes back in when a player or Game Master order arrives, before the order takes over. Before, BBB let go of the hatch with him still out and never took it back, so he rode out the fight exposed, and stayed out after the order ended. He turns out again only after the usual 30 quiet seconds once the order is over (-bbbCrewTurnout 0).
- For mod makers: a host mod can tell BBB that a group's player-level order is its own, not a player's (new hook `HostOrder`). Some hosts run their AI commander's urgent moves at the player's level so they outrank combat. Under such an order, the crew of an armed vehicle now stay in their seats at gates and when a crewmate needs healing, as they do under an ordinary order. Real player and Game Master orders are still left alone.
- Base-game AI fix: no more script error when a squad spots an enemy that has no faction (a NULL pointer to `targetFaction` while marking it on the map). That enemy just isn't marked on the map.

## 0.1.6 — released

Squads that fight as a team: they share what they see over the radio, commit to a plan, flank and search, and pull back properly when they break. Player and Game Master orders still come first.

- Gunners stay on held guns. A host mod can now tell BBB which static guns a squad holds where it stands, such as a checkpoint MG or a dug-in position (new hook `GetHeldGuns`; plain vanilla and Game Master groups hold none). On those guns the gunner no longer climbs out because an enemy he heard about or saw a while ago is outside the gun's arc: he only gets out for an enemy he saw within 60 m in the last 10 seconds. If a held gun stands empty for 8 seconds, the squad's nearest soldier on foot within 40 m mans it again. BBB's own static crewing keeps a held gun manned when its sector goes quiet. Vehicles are unchanged (-bbbHoldGuns 0).
- Base-game AI fixes: no more script errors when a group's orders are replaced while it is defending ("Wrong class of provided Waypoint!", "Defend Waypoint not provided to the node!"), or when a group without a leader just then fails a move (a NULL pointer in `SCR_AIProcessFailedMovementResult`). These hit any host or Game Master who changes a defending group's orders. The nodes now fail quietly, which is what the errors ended in anyway, so the AI behaves the same.
- Squads warn each other by radio. When a squad spots enemies, friendly squads within its radio range (about 1.3 km with personal radios, 2 km with a manpack) hear about it a few seconds later and react as if they'd heard the shooting: they turn to it, take cover or send a fireteam to look. A report is only where the enemy was last seen, never a target to shoot at, and it isn't passed on again. Squads without a radio only hear each other within shouting distance. Player-led squads and squads under a player's or Game Master's order are left alone (-bbbContactRelay 0).
- Squads can make bounded reactions to nearby contacts during ordinary AI movement orders, then continue their route. Explicit player/GM orders and transport remain in charge. Use -bbbOrderLeash 0 to retain the previous waypoint restrictions.
- Soldiers out of magazines go and get more, even mid-fight. A rifleman with no spare magazine heads for the nearest friendly body with matching ammo instead of carrying on the firefight or being sent forward in a bound, then rejoins his team. Switching to his pistol no longer makes him forget he needs ammo (-bbbRearm 0).
- Squads go round a dug-in enemy. Facing an enemy who stays put 120 to 250 m away, a squad first holds and fires to fix him, then flanks: one half runs to covered ground well off his line while the other half covers it, swapping as they go, and they attack from the new angle. When contact is lost, a squad searches: half of it checks where the enemy was last seen and the cover around it, one spot at a time, while the other half watches, then calls clear. Covering fire is now aimed at the tree, rock or wall nearest where the enemy was seen, and never near friendly soldiers (-bbbTacticFlank 0, -bbbTacticSearch 0, -bbbCoverFire 0).
- Squads commit to a plan. On first contact a squad pauses to take in the enemy, then its leader picks a plan and sticks with it: assault (fire and manoeuvre toward the enemy, about 1.5 minutes) when the odds are fair, or hold and fight from cover (about 45 seconds) when it is outnumbered, flanked, pinned down or the enemy is far off. A squad that finds more enemies than it thought switches from assault to holding. When the leader falls, the squad holds for 20 seconds while someone takes over. The leader calls the plan out loud: move, defend this position, fall back. Squads under a player's or Game Master's order are left alone (-bbbTactic 0).
- Squads read a fight from two sides correctly. A squad facing enemies in more than one direction now aims its smoke, its static gun and its fire-and-manoeuvre at the group that matters most (the nearest, freshest and biggest it has seen) instead of at an empty spot between them, and a squad that breaks retreats through the open side instead of backing away from one group straight into the other (-bbbSquadPicture 0).
- Squads that break really pull back. A squad whose morale breaks now falls back in two halves: one half runs while the other holds its ground and keeps firing, then they swap, even under fire. Before, the runners often never moved and the covering half advanced on the enemy. A lone survivor runs instead of standing still. Losing the squad leader now counts toward a break for a while after someone else takes over (-bbbMorale 0).
- One job at a time. A soldier dragging or treating a wounded buddy, walking to a static gun, or fetching ammo is no longer counted into a fire-and-manoeuvre bound, sent indoors, or picked to throw a grenade or smoke at the same moment. A rescue still takes him from anything else (-bbbClaims 0).
- Vehicle crews stay in their seats. Drivers no longer climb out under fire to open a gate (BBB opens it for them), and the driver, gunner or commander of an armed or armoured vehicle no longer bails out to treat a wounded buddy: someone on foot goes instead. Double gates now open for AI vehicles too, and armour that is under fire or near enemies opens gates well ahead so it doesn't slow down (-bbbCrewStays 0).

## 0.1.5 — released

More initiative under fire, with your orders still in charge. This update enables BBB's combat, medical and vehicle features by default; each retains its own server off switch. The optional Think panel remains off.

### Fire and movement

- Fireteams alternate covering fire and movement. Covering soldiers can suppress a recently reported enemy position after the enemy ducks, movers tolerate brief pauses in cover fire, and a casualty no longer ends the whole manoeuvre. Use -bbbFireManeuver 0 to disable it, or -bbbFireCoverOrders 0 to disable only the ordered covering fire.
- Squads seek indoor cover near detected enemies and investigate further reachable positions when contacts are inside. Blocked routes are abandoned and player orders take priority. This is contact-driven movement, not a guarantee of clearing every room (-bbbBuildings 0 disables).
- Soldiers use carried grenades against nearby enemies in cover, checking friendly clearance and the throwing route before committing. Squad cooldowns limit repeated throws (-bbbGrenades 0).
- Idle or defending soldiers can walk to a nearby loaded friendly static weapon and crew it during contact. They give it up for orders, exhausted ammunition or a lost target (-bbbStaticWeapons 0).
- Badly battered autonomous squads can withdraw in covered bounds; isolated, out-of-ammo survivors cornered by an enemy can surrender. Withdrawals stop if the squad cannot make real progress (-bbbMorale 0).

### Keep each other alive

- Tactical smoke screens casualty drags, advances and withdrawals toward known threat sectors (-bbbSmoke 0). Fixed medic smoke being skipped before the throw could happen.
- Safer healing is now enabled by default (-bbbMedic 0 disables). Medics fight a nearby visible enemy before approaching a casualty, and casualty draggers let go to return fire against an immediate threat.
- Medics no longer backtrack more than 75 m to a casualty during a fight. They can still go when the squad closes up or the fighting quiets down.
- Suppressed soldiers caught without cover keep spotting and fighting back; soldiers in cover still keep their heads down (-bbbExposedFire 0).
- Riflemen no longer permanently give up on jeeps and Humvees after their shots strike the hull. Only real armour triggers rifle-immunity handling. The get-low reaction now requires recently seen armour, while AT specialists retain their firing stance (-bbbArmourScatter 0).
- Night illumination uses carried flare ammunition to light recently sighted enemy sectors, with squad and area cooldowns to limit repeated launches (-bbbFlares 0).

### Crews, doors and squad cohesion

- Supported armoured crew positions turn out after a quiet period and button up when danger returns (-bbbCrewTurnout 0).
- Idle wheeled-vehicle drivers can make a small, guarded adjustment to bring a gunner's visible target inside a limited turret arc. Player driving, movement orders and unsafe ground take priority (-bbbDriverAlign 0).
- Against launcher fire, supported vehicles use fitted smoke weapons; slow wheeled vehicles can also make a short guarded reverse (-bbbArmorReaction 0).
- Turret-linked commander seats count as crew, keeping their occupants aboard when passengers dismount under fire (-bbbCommanderCrew 0).
- Soldiers stuck at a house door while trying to move can swing it fully open or flip it to the other side. Doors are never closed on anyone (-bbbDoorUnstick 0).
- Squads use an awake member as their reference when their leader is knocked out. Soldiers bounding or withdrawing are no longer diverted to scavenge or crew static weapons.
- Fixed a weapon-mode issue that let AI aim a very fast-firing weapon without ever firing.

### See the decision, share the feedback

- Hosts and mission makers can enable -bbbThink 1 and look at an AI soldier to see BBB's decisions and reasons for holding back. Use -bbbThink 2 to include the notes in the server log; the panel is off by default.
